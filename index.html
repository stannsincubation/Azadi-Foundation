About Azadi Foundation

Catalyzing Deep-Tech & Industrial Growth

Azadi Foundation for Innovation & Incubation is a Section-8 non-profit startup incubator committed to nurturing high-impact technology ventures. We bridge the critical gap between research, prototyping, and enterprise creation.

Backed by 14+ years of leadership experience in academia, Industry 4.0, and social development, we provide founders with world-class facilities, prototyping capabilities, and grant mentorship.

Our Mission

Provide end-to-end incubation, R&D labs, and seed grant support for technology founders.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable regional and national ecosystem for deep-tech and social innovation.

Dr. Naveen Vadde
